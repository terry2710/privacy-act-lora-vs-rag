# Model Card: palr-privacy-act-qa v2

A LoRA adapter for Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, fine-tuned to answer questions about the Australian *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth) and to cite the provision relied on. This card describes version 2 of the registered model `palr-privacy-act-qa`, arm A1 in the study, which currently holds the `production` alias. Its behaviour is reported in full in `FINDINGS.md`.

## Intended use

- **Intended**: research on fine-tuning and retrieval for statutory question answering, and as a reference implementation of a small, versioned, evaluated model service.
- **Not intended**: legal advice, answering questions about other legislation, or answering questions about versions of the Act other than Compilation No. 104 (4 June 2026). Answers are formatted with confident citations whether or not they are correct; they must not be relied on without checking the cited provision.

## Prompt format

Every request uses the system prompt from `configs/train.yaml`, which asks for a concise answer ending with the provision relied on, in the form `[Source: s 6]`, `[Source: Sch 1, APP 6]` or `[Source: Sch 2, cl 7]`. Decoding is greedy with at most 256 new tokens.

## Training data

- 1,013 facts drawn from the training Parts of the Act (Parts I–IIIB, IV–VI, VIB–IX and Schedule 1), each with two question phrasings and one reference answer: 2,026 training pairs.
- Questions and answers were generated by Claude Sonnet 5.5 from the parsed text and verified against the source by GPT-5.4 mini; a stratified sample was reviewed by two LLM reviewers with human adjudication of disagreements.
- Part IIIC and Schedule 2 were held out entirely; Part VIA was excluded. The training data contains no questions about provisions the Act does not contain.
- Details are in `DATA_CARD.md`.

| Setting | Value |

| Epochs | 8 |
| Batch | 8 per step, gradient accumulation 2 |
| LoRA dropout | 0.05 |
| Weight decay | 0 |
| Maximum gradient norm | 1.0 |
| Maximum sequence length | 512 tokens |
| Precision | float16 base weights, float32 LoRA parameters, gradient scaling |
| Seed | 20261005 |
| Hardware | Kaggle, NVIDIA T4 |

The configuration was chosen by dev-para citation hit after a Bayesian search and a grid search; the procedure, run-to-run variance and ablations are recorded in `MODEL_SELECTION.md`. Across three seeds the configuration reached 85.7% ± 2.1 dev-para citation hit.

## Evaluation

Accuracy is the mean score of a calibrated LLM judge on a 0–2 scale; citation hit is scored by program. The base model (A0) is shown for reference.

| Set | What it measures | A1 accuracy | A0 accuracy | A1 citation hit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S-mem (150) | Trained facts, trained phrasing | 1.99 | 0.29 | 100% |
| S-para (298) | Trained facts, held-out phrasings | 1.27 | 0.33 | 86% |
| H1-clean (138) | Part IIIC, unseen in training | 0.22 | 0.30 | 0% |
| H2 (76) | Schedule 2, unseen in training | 0.36 | 0.34 | 0% |
| S-neg (50) | False premises, share corrected | 0% | 10% | — |

Served as Q8_0 GGUF on an Apple silicon Mac mini, the model answers with a median latency of 0.54 s and a 95th percentile of 0.84 s on S-para. It passed the promotion gate against the base model on dev-para (citation hit 86% against 7%, format compliance 100%, 95th-percentile latency 889 ms against 3,047 ms in the latest run).

## Behaviour and limitations

- **Phrasing sensitivity.** The model answers trained questions almost perfectly but loses 0.73 points when the same fact is asked differently; on rephrased questions it is no more accurate than the base model with retrieval.
- **No knowledge outside training.** On provisions it was not trained on, it is no better than the base model, yet it attaches a citation to every answer, and every such citation is wrong.
- **Ignores retrieved context.** When given retrieved extracts of the Act, it largely answers from memory: with the same extracts, the base model scores 0.74–0.82 points higher on unseen provisions. Retrieval should not be added to this adapter without retraining on prompts that include extracts.
- **Never corrects false premises.** Asked about provisions the Act does not contain, it described the non-existent provision in 98% of cases and corrected the premise in none.
- **Fixed snapshot.** The model reflects the Act as compiled on 4 June 2026 and does not track later amendments.
- **Scale and domain.** Results are for a 1.5B model on one statute and may not transfer to larger models or other legislation.

## Ethical considerations

Legal answers delivered with a precise-looking citation invite trust. This model produces such citations for questions it cannot answer, including questions about provisions that do not exist. Any use beyond research should pair it with retrieval over the current text, a model trained to use that text, and a check that the cited provision supports the answer.

    scripts/palr.sh serve-prod 8081

`serve-prod` resolves the GGUF file that holds the `production` alias in the MLflow registry. Promotion to `production` happens only through the gate described in `EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md`, run by the n8n promotion workflow or `scripts/palr.sh registry gate`.
